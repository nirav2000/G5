# Marking and official documentation
The official ABRSM Grade 5 online Music Theory exam is computer-marked, with seven sections and 75 total marks. The online candidate receives a digital PDF mark form with marks by section, available from the ABRSM account after results are issued. The practical/performance examiner-written form is **not** the appropriate template for Grade 5 online theory.

Official information:
- https://www.abrsm.org/en-cy/results-and-certificates
- https://www.abrsm.org/en-ai/about-our-exams/exam-regulations
- https://www.abrsm.org/sites/default/files/2023-09/abrsm-ot-reference-sheet-example-questions_online-music-theory-from-2020.pdf

A blank official assessor form was not located as a publicly licensed distributable asset. Do not misrepresent the original G5 practice score sheet as an official mark form. Parents may download their own official form after an actual exam.
